Config: BossBegoman

A large Topman boss. Appears from the sky with a cutscene when battle is activated. In order to defeat it, the player must jump on its head and spin attack it to push it into ElectricRail objects that surround his arena. In SMG1, it will always spawn a Power Star when defeated, which requires a properly configured Scenario entry.
